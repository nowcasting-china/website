The annual rate is the mean of the four quarterly YoY rates: released quarters at the official number, open quarters at the model estimate. A release's impact on the annual rate is its impact on each decomposed quarter divided by four; a GDP release also carries the switch from the model's backcast to the official quarterly number. The remainder is the change in the estimate not explained by the listed releases: re-estimation of the model between updates, data revisions, and any open quarter not decomposed at that date. The same definition is used on the nowcast tab.

Model results only: the released data values are not shown. The model is estimated on releases of the NBS, the Ministry of Transport, the General Administration of Customs and S&P Global. Panel: IP, electricity, construction, exports, freight, NBS and S&P Global PMIs, retail sales, non-manufacturing PMI, business climate index, GDP.
